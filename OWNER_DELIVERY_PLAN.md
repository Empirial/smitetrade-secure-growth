# Owner Delivery Plan

## Objective
Provide owners with a realistic week-by-week plan to reach a polished retail-core launch.

Launch scope:

- Owner
- Cashier
- Customer
- Admin

Deferred:

- Driver
- Lender
- advanced credit product rollout

## Estimated Duration
5 to 8 weeks

## Week-By-Week Plan

## Week 1: Audit And Scope Lock

Focus:

- confirm exactly what is in launch scope
- identify blockers across retail-core portals
- define what is postponed

Outputs:

- launch scope document
- blocker list
- owner alignment on phased delivery

## Week 2: Owner And Cashier Stabilization

Focus:

- inventory
- POS
- checkout
- staff
- orders
- receipts
- shifts

Outputs:

- stable retail operations baseline
- first pass of high-priority bug fixes

## Week 3: Customer Flow Stabilization

Focus:

- product browsing
- cart
- checkout
- payment
- order history
- customer profile basics

Outputs:

- stable customer purchase journey
- reduced friction in buyer-facing flows

## Week 4: Admin Validation And Platform Control

Focus:

- users
- stores
- audit logs
- alerts
- analytics
- support and oversight views

Outputs:

- owner/admin operational visibility
- higher confidence in platform controls

## Week 5: Permissions, Security, And Regression Pass

Focus:

- role validation
- tenant isolation checks
- cross-role action testing
- regression fixing from shared context logic

Outputs:

- stronger production confidence
- reduced security and data-access risk

## Week 6: UX Polish And UAT Prep

Focus:

- content cleanup
- unfinished-state cleanup
- loading and empty states
- demo and owner-facing polish

Outputs:

- UAT-ready build candidate
- cleaner presentation to stakeholders

## Week 7: UAT And Bug Fixing

Focus:

- walkthrough testing with business users
- fix defects found during acceptance testing
- revalidate critical flows

Outputs:

- signoff candidate
- final release issue list

## Week 8: Release Preparation

Focus:

- final smoke testing
- deployment preparation
- rollback and support checklist
- owner launch signoff

Outputs:

- release-ready plan
- launch checklist

## If Faster Delivery Is Needed
Compressed version:

- 5 to 6 weeks

This requires:

- strict scope control
- no Driver or Lender work
- no major redesigns
- quick owner decisions
- focus only on launch-critical bugs

## If Higher Confidence Is Needed
Expanded version:

- 7 to 8 weeks

This allows:

- more QA
- more UAT cycles
- better polish
- safer release preparation

## Owner-Friendly Summary
The project does not need to wait for every future portal to be complete.

Best delivery approach:

1. launch the retail-core first
2. prove traction and operations
3. finish Driver next
4. finish Lender and advanced credit after that

## Dependencies For Success

- timely owner feedback
- clear launch scope discipline
- working test and deployment environment
- consistent prioritization of P0 and P1 issues
